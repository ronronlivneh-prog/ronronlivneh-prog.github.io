# Erez Education Venture and SEO Design

## Intent

Strengthen the official site as the primary source for searches about Ron Livneh and Erez Livneh, while expanding Erez's profile with his educational entrepreneurship and the Singular School project.

## Goals

- Add a concise, visible section about Erez founding the Singular School project, including a descriptive external link to `https://singularity-schools.com/`.
- Make page titles, descriptions and visible headings consistently identify Ron and Erez with their lecture topics.
- Add truthful structured data that helps search engines understand the two profile pages and the lecture pages.
- Strengthen crawling and discovery through internal links, canonical URLs, `robots.txt` and the XML sitemap.
- Keep the current GitHub Pages URLs canonical until `livnehtalks.co.il` is purchased and connected.

## Non-goals

- No guarantee of first position in Google.
- No hidden text, keyword stuffing or misuse of competing agencies' brand names.
- No Search Console verification until the custom domain is available or the user chooses to verify the GitHub property.
- No blog requirement in this phase.

## Approach

Use an entity-first SEO approach rather than creating thin keyword pages. The official profile pages will use `ProfilePage` with a `Person` main entity, while lecture pages retain `Service` data and add `BreadcrumbList`. Titles, descriptions, Open Graph data and visible content will use the same language so Google receives consistent signals.

## Content constraints

- The Singular School claim must remain consistent with the project's own site: a unique educational program founded by Erez Livneh, inspired by his participation in Singularity University, and recognized with two national education awards.
- Public HTML must not use en dashes or em dashes.
- Existing page design, contact routes and speaker separation remain unchanged.
